Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Notice for Mutun ("we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you download and use our mobile application (Mutun).

Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at moawya14362@gmail.com.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal information you disclose to us: We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services. The personal information we collect includes:

  • Full Name
  • Personal Identification Number (PIN) / Password
  • Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Age and Gender
  • Country / Nationality

App Usage & Performance Data: As you use the Mutun app to memorize texts, we collect data regarding your progress, quiz scores, audio recitation grades, daily streaks, and leaderboard rankings.

Information automatically collected: We automatically collect certain information when you visit, use, or navigate the Services. This includes device and usage information, such as your IP address, browser and device characteristics, operating system, and information about how and when you use our Services (via Firebase Analytics) to maintain security and improve app performance.

2. How Do We Process Your Information?

We process your personal information for a variety of reasons, depending on how you interact with our Services, including:

  • To facilitate account creation and authentication: So you can log in securely using your Serial Number (SN) and PIN from any device.
  • To deliver services to the user: To track your memorization progress, calculate your grades, and generate certificates of completion.
  • To manage the Leaderboard: Your name and score will be processed to display your ranking on the competitive student leaderboards.

3. Do We Offer Artificial Intelligence-Based Products?

Yes. As part of our Services, we offer features powered by artificial intelligence, specifically the Smart AI Tutor and the Automated Audio Recitation Grader.

Audio Data Processing: When you use the audio recitation feature, your voice recording is temporarily transmitted to our AI Service Provider (Google Gemini API) to grade your recitation, identify mistakes, and provide feedback. We do not permanently store your raw audio files on our servers. Only the resulting transcript, grade, and feedback are saved to your profile.

Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy. You must not use the AI Products in any way that violates the terms or policies of our AI Service Providers.

4. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Data?

We may share your data with third-party vendors and service providers who perform services for us or on our behalf. We have contracts in place with these third parties to help safeguard your personal information. The categories of third parties we share personal information with include:

  • Cloud Computing & Database Services: We use Google Firebase to host our database, authenticate users, and store your app progress securely.
  • AI Service Providers: We use Google Cloud AI (Gemini) to process text explanations and evaluate audio recitations.
  • Data Analytics Services: We use Google Analytics for Firebase to track crash reports and analyze app usage.

5. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We keep your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Notice unless otherwise required by law. No purpose in this notice will require us to keep your personal information for longer than the period of time in which you have an active account with us. When we have no ongoing legitimate business need to process your personal information, we will either delete or anonymize it.

While we strive to protect your personal information, transmitting it to and from our services is at your own risk.
