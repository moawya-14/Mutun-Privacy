Mutun Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Mutun ("we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal information when you use our application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating or updating an account, using our services or contacting us. This may include:

  • Account information: Your name, student number, sign-in credentials and gender.
  • Optional profile information: Your email address, phone number, age and nationality or country.
  • Learning information: Course participation, memorization progress, assessment results, achievements and completion records.
  • Voice information: Audio and recognized speech when you choose to use recitation features, as described below.
  • Technical information: IP addresses, device and application information, connection records and security-related activity processed when you access our services.
  • Communications: Information you include in support enquiries or other messages to us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and manage accounts, verify identity and provide our services.
  • Maintain learning records, provide assessments and issue completion certificates.
  • Display student rankings and achievements.
  • Provide reminders, service announcements and customer support.
  • Analyze participation and service use, improve educational content and support, and understand students' needs.
  • Protect accounts, prevent misuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

We may analyze account and learning information, including email addresses, phone numbers and nationality where relevant to these purposes. Where practical, we use aggregated or de-identified information for reporting and improvement.

3. Voice and Microphone Access

Recitation features require your permission to access the microphone. Your speech is processed for transcription and assessment. Depending on your device and recognition service, audio may be transmitted to Google, Apple or another installed speech-recognition provider. Their privacy policies and your device settings govern their processing and retention of that audio.

Mutun does not retain raw recitation recordings on its servers. We retain assessment results and word-level feedback as part of your learning record, rather than the full recognized transcript. You can stop recitation or revoke microphone and speech-recognition permission through your device settings.

For further information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Speech Recognition privacy information.

4. How We Share Information

We may share personal information with third parties where necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including:

  • Service providers: Providers of hosting, data storage, account authentication, speech recognition, support and information analysis. The information shared may include account details, email addresses, phone numbers, nationality and learning records where relevant to the service provided.
  • Authorized academy staff: Staff who need access to manage courses, student accounts and support requests.
  • Leaderboard viewers: Your display name, score, rank and limited completion information may be visible to other users or the public. Leaderboards do not display your PIN, email address or phone number.
  • Recipients you choose: People, applications or services with whom you choose to share a certificate or other information.
  • Legal and security recipients: Authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect users, rights or the security of our services.

We limit disclosures to information relevant to the stated purpose. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

5. Storage, Retention and Security

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the services, maintain your account and fulfil the purposes described in this policy. Certain records may be retained where necessary for legal obligations, security, dispute resolution or backup recovery.

Some information is stored on your device to support offline access. Exported files and copies shared with others remain at their chosen destinations until removed. Information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence by us or our service providers.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You can edit available profile information, leave optional fields blank, manage reminders and change device permissions. Notifications may appear on your lock screen according to your device settings.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, obtain a copy of or delete your information, or to restrict or object to certain processing. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent. Contact us to exercise your rights; we may need to verify your identity. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Delete your account: Open Settings → Delete account in the application, enter your PIN and confirm. An internet connection is required. Deletion removes your active account and associated profile and learning records.

Alternatively, email moawya14362@gmail.com with the subject "Mutun account deletion request" and your student number. Do not send your PIN or password by email.

Previously published or downloaded leaderboard copies may take time to refresh. Contact us about an outdated published listing. Deletion does not remove files you exported, copies retained by others or data held independently by third-party services.

7. Children's Privacy

Parents and guardians may contact us about a child's personal information or request its correction or deletion. If you believe a child has provided information without any consent required by applicable law, please contact us so we can review and address the matter.

8. Third-Party Links

Our services may link to external websites or services. Their handling of personal information is governed by their own privacy policies. Please review those policies when using them.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services, data practices or legal requirements. The latest revision date appears above. We will communicate material changes through an appropriate notice.
